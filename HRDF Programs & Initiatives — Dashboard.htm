i
How this is calculated. During Tamheer, HRDF funds the trainee allowance, so the host saves the equivalent loaded staff cost (basic, housing, transport, GOSI, insurance = SAR 23,234/month). The headline figure counts the 36 trainees who completed a full six-month placement. Across all 80 enrolled trainees the six-month saving would be SAR 11,152,320.
